# 📊 Global Superstore Sales Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset using SQL, Excel, and Power BI to understand sales performance, customer behavior, regional performance, and shipping trends. The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data validation, KPI analysis, and interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products, categories, customers, and regions.
- Evaluate monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Analyze customer segments and shipping performance.
- Build interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- December recorded the highest monthly sales.
- The United States was the top-performing country.
- The Central region generated the highest revenue.
- Consumer was the largest customer segment.
- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing regions and markets.
- Improve shipping efficiency in high-cost markets.
- Develop targeted campaigns for different customer segments.
